Make a full song in 5 steps

  1. Pick a genre. Click New and choose Worship, Rap/Trap, Hip-Hop, Jazz, Classical, Gospel, Pop, Afrobeats or R&B. You get a complete song structure (intro, verse, chorus…) with drums, chords, bass and pads already playing.
  2. Shape the beat in Beat Maker. Each section (Verse, Chorus…) has its own drum pattern, chord progression and melody.
    • Drums: click a square to add a hit. Shift+click = soft ghost note. Right-click = hi-hat roll (2, 3 or 4 quick hits). Drag to paint.
    • Chords: type Roman numerals so the song can change key easily, e.g. I | V | vi | IV. Put two chords in one bar with a space: ii7 V7. Plain names like Am | F | C | G work too. You can also pick one from the progression list.
    • Melody: click to place a note. Drag to the right to make it longer. Click a note again to delete it.
    • Change the sounds (kit, keys, pad, bass, melody) and the playing style (held chords, arpeggio, walking bass, 808…).
  3. Arrange the song in Arrange & Record. Add, remove and reorder sections in Song structure. When you edit a section, every place it's used in the song changes too. Use Make unique if you want one copy to be different.
  4. Record your voice. Wear headphones. Click on the timeline where you want to start, then press ● (or R). You get one bar of count-in clicks, then you're recording. Press ● or ■ to stop. Every take goes on the armed track (the one with the red ● lit). Add more tracks for harmonies, ad-libs or instruments. You can also Import audio files.
    • Edit a take: drag it to move it. Drag its left or right edge to trim. Drag the small top-corner handles to fade in or out. S splits it at the playhead. Ctrl+D duplicates it. Delete removes it.
    • If your takes sound late or early, adjust Latency in the toolbar. Then re-record, or just slide the clip.
  5. Mix & master. Use the vocal presets (Clean, Rap, Worship, Radio, Choir), or set EQ, compression, reverb, delay, volume and pan per track. On the master, turn on Glue and raise Loudness until it sounds full but not distorted. Then click Export song to get a WAV file. You can also export the instrumental only or the vocals only.
